# Good Turn Everyday

Cheap flights, a day out anywhere, jobs, clothes, football and books.

Connects these remote MCP servers (no sign-in, read-only):

- **Fare Deals** (`faredeals` server): Cheap flights from any city, to one place or anywhere, with the cheapest days to fly.
- **Spin My Day** (`spinmyday` server): Plans a whole day out in any city, matched to the weather, your mood and diet.
- **Job Spotter** (`jobspotter` server): Search current US and UK job listings by role and place, title matches first.
- **Wardrobe Connect** (`wardrobeconnect` server): Find clothes that match a description or a picture on eBay, new and pre-owned.
- **Form Guide** (`formguide` server): Football form, fixtures, results and tables for six top European leagues.
- **Books Like This** (`bookslikethis` server): Books like one you enjoyed, a book's details and any film of it, and an author's books.

Privacy: https://goodturn-mcp.pages.dev/privacy
