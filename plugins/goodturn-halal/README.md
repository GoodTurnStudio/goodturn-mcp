# Good Turn Halal

Halal checks for food, medicines, stocks, funds and crypto, and Shariah screening of US stocks under four standards.

Connects these remote MCP servers (no sign-in, read-only):

- **Halal or Not** (`halalornot` server): Is it halal? Food, medicines, stocks, funds and crypto, with a verdict for each of the four schools.
- **Mizan** (`mizan` server): Shariah screening of US stocks under four standards, halal alternatives, purification and zakat.

Privacy: https://goodturn-mcp.pages.dev/privacy
