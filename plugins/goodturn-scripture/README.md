# Good Turn Scripture

Daily readings, explanations and whole books from the Talmud, Gurbani, Bhagavad Gita, Bible, Greek and Norse myth and the Stoics.

Connects these remote MCP servers (no sign-in, read-only):

- **Talmud Scroller** (`talmud` server): Talmud a few lines a day, explained, with today's Daf Yomi, search and tractates in English.
- **Scroll the Gurbani** (`gurbani` server): Gurbani a few lines a day in Gurmukhi with English, any Ang or shabad, and whole banis.
- **Gita Scroller** (`gita` server): The Bhagavad Gita a few verses a day in Sanskrit with English, any verse, and the whole Gita.
- **Bible Swipe** (`bible` server): A Bible reading a day, line by line, any passage by reference, and search.
- **Scroll the Lore** (`lore` server): A Greek or Norse myth a day, explained, and Homer, Hesiod and the Poetic Edda to read.
- **Stoic Scroller** (`stoics` server): Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus and Seneca a few lines a day, and their whole books to read.
